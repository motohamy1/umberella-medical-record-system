# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Clinic staff (physicians, specialists, nurses, and receptionists) in hospital and ambulatory clinical environments. In the midst of patient encounters or triage, clinicians need to understand the patient's complete longitudinal clinical history in seconds without fragmented tab-switching. Patients also access a focused companion view for upcoming care, lab readings, and care plans.

## Product Purpose

A dual-sided clinical electronic health record system centered on a tactile, paper-metaphor Patient Clinical Workspace. The primary workspace provides a high-density, 8-tab stepladder navigation, an interactive continuous care timeline projecting clinical milestones and medication events, and an immediate clinical summary (diagnosis, vital signs, allergies, and care pathways).

## Brand Personality

- **Voice & Tone**: Precise, clinical, tactile, restrained, authoritative.
- **Three-Word Personality**: Tactile, Clinical, Grounded.
- **Emotional Goal**: Fosters clinical confidence, calmness, and immediate spatial orientation under high cognitive load.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS templates with floating cards and soft wide drop shadows.
- Unanchored AI-generated dashboard tropes (e.g. gratuitous gradient text, arbitrary numbered eyebrows).
- Cream/beige parchment slop masquerading as paper — must use authentic slate/stone tinted medical neutrals.
- Disconnected data silos or hidden clinical changes requiring multiple modal dialogs.
- Raw hex color overrides and out-of-system styles that violate token discipline.

## Design Principles

1. **Tactile Paper Metaphor**: Physical clinical folder and descending stepladder tabs with precise notched surfaces, hairlines, and authentic density.
2. **Direct Spatial Hierarchy**: Clinical status, allergies, diagnosis, and vital signs immediately visible without scrolling or modal interruption.
3. **Continuous Temporal Projection**: Longitudinal clinical timeline displaying milestones, medication events, and deltas across care visits.
4. **Token Discipline & Contrast**: Strict reliance on semantic design tokens with high legibility (≥4.5:1 body contrast) and zero arbitrary hardcoded styling.
5. **Bilingual Grounding**: First-class support for both English (LTR) and Arabic (RTL) typography and layout flow.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG 2.1 AA compliance across all clinical surfaces.
- High-contrast text on clinical slate/stone surfaces.
- Full keyboard navigation for tabbed stepladder switching and timeline scrubber.
- Native RTL layout support with IBM Plex Sans Arabic.
- Respect for `prefers-reduced-motion` across timeline expansions and state transitions.
